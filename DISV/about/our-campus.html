Our Campus

Home · About · Our Campus

Danube International School Vienna is located at Josef Gall-Gasse 2 in Vienna's vibrant 2nd district (Leopoldstadt), within easy reach of the Donaukanal and the historic city centre. The campus is well served by public transport, making it accessible for families from across the city and the wider Vienna region.

Our purpose-built facilities are designed to support every aspect of an IB education. Modern, well-resourced classrooms provide stimulating environments for inquiry-based learning from Early Years through Grade 12. Dedicated science laboratories equipped for both Junior and Senior programmes give students hands-on experience with experimental science from an early age.

A spacious sports hall supports our active PE and after-school sports programme, while our library and media centre is a hub for research, reading, and collaborative learning. The school cafeteria offers a welcoming space for students to relax and refuel, with healthy and varied meal options every day.

Above all, DISV is a place where students feel safe, welcomed, and known. Our compact, community-focused campus means that students from Early Years and Grade 12 share spaces, events, and traditions — creating a genuine sense of belonging across all year groups.
