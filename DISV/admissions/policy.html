Admissions Policy

Home / Admissions / Admissions Policy

Open Enrolment

DISV is committed to providing access to high-quality international education regardless of nationality, religion, ethnicity or background. We welcome students from all over the world and strive to create an inclusive learning environment where every child can thrive.

Eligibility

DISV is suitable for students who will benefit from an English-medium international education. No prior knowledge of English is required — our English as an Additional Language (EAL) programme provides targeted support to help new learners integrate quickly and confidently into school life.

Non-Discrimination Statement

DISV does not discriminate on the basis of nationality, race, religion, gender or disability. Every application is considered on its individual merits, and we are committed to creating a welcoming school community that reflects the diversity of the world we live in.

Priority Enrolment

Siblings of current DISV students receive priority consideration during the admissions process. When places are available, offers are made in order of completed applications. We encourage families to apply early to secure a place, though rolling admissions means we consider new applications throughout the school year.
