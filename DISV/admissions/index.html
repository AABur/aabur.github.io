Admissions process, fees and policy

Choosing the right school is one of the most important decisions for any family — and at Danube International School Vienna, we make the process as personal, transparent, and supportive as possible. Our Admissions Team is here to guide you every step of the way, whether you are moving to Vienna for the first time or transitioning from another school.

We welcome applications from students of all nationalities and backgrounds who are eager to learn, grow, and contribute to our international community. As an IB World School, DISV offers a continuous, high-quality education from Early Years Unit through Grade 12.

We take time to get to know each family personally, ensuring DISV is the right fit for your child's academic, social, and emotional growth. Rolling admissions means we welcome students at any time during the school year.

How It Works

Four steps to joining DISV

1. Enquire

Contact our Registrar to discuss your child's needs, year group, and any questions about school life at DISV. We will help you understand whether our programme is the right fit.

2. Visit

We invite all prospective families for a campus tour and meeting with our admissions team. Experience the welcoming spirit of our school firsthand.

3. Apply

Complete the online application form and submit the required documents including previous school reports, language assessments, and references.

4. Join

Once your application is reviewed and a place is confirmed, our team will guide you through enrollment, welcome you to the community, and ensure a smooth start.
