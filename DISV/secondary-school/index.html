IB Middle Years & Diploma

Excellence in international education

DISV's Secondary School offers a rigorous, internationally recognised education through the IB Middle Years Programme (Grades 6–10) and Diploma Programme (Grades 11–12). Our students develop academic depth, intercultural understanding, and the personal resilience needed to succeed at leading universities and in an interconnected world.
