Request Fee Information

Contact Yvonne Wiedeschitz at registrar@danubeschool.com for current tuition details. Fees are published annually at the beginning of each school year.

What the Fee Includes

DISV tuition fees cover full access to the IB programme, qualified teaching, learning materials, IT resources, and a range of school activities. Students benefit from a low student-to-staff ratio of 5:1 and dedicated pastoral support throughout their time at DISV.

Additional Costs

Certain costs are not included in the annual tuition fee. These may include after-school activities and clubs, school trips and expeditions, meals and catering, uniform and stationery, and privately taught languages. The Registrar can provide a full breakdown on request.
