Primary Years Programme

A foundation for lifelong learning

At DISV, our Elementary School is built around the IB Primary Years Programme — an inquiry-led framework that nurtures curiosity, creativity, and international-mindedness from Early Years through Grade 5. Our experienced teachers guide children to ask big questions, make meaningful connections, and develop a genuine love of learning that lasts a lifetime.
