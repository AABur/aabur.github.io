30+ Activities for Secondary Students

Life at DISV extends well beyond the classroom. Our after-school activities programme offers Secondary students a wide range of clubs and activities to explore passions, develop skills, and build the connections and experiences that shape character.

  • Sports — football, swimming, tennis, basketball, volleyball, and fitness training, with inter-school competitions and tournaments
  • Arts — music ensembles, school productions, drama workshops, and visual arts clubs supporting creative development and performance
  • STEM — coding, robotics, Science Olympiad, and technology projects for students interested in science, engineering, and innovation
  • Model United Nations (MUN) — debate, diplomacy, and research skills through participation in school and international MUN conferences
  • Community Service — student-led service projects addressing local and global needs, contributing to CAS requirements for Diploma students

Activities run on weekday afternoons and are open to all Secondary students. For the current schedule and registration details, please contact the school office.
