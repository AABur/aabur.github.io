The DISV Community

Parents, alumni and shared purpose

At Danube International School Vienna, community is at the heart of who we are. With families and staff representing over 60 nationalities, DISV is a place where cultures connect, friendships flourish, and every individual feels a genuine sense of belonging.

Our school community extends far beyond the classroom. Parents, students, and teachers work together in an atmosphere of mutual respect and shared purpose — celebrating achievements, supporting one another, and creating lasting memories through school events, performances, and international celebrations.

The Parent Association of Danube International School (PADIS) plays a vital role in school life, organising social gatherings, cultural events, and fundraising initiatives that strengthen our connections. Students engage in community and service projects that encourage empathy, leadership, and a sense of global responsibility.

Whether it's cheering on classmates at sports day, collaborating on a service-learning project, or coming together for our annual International Day, life at DISV is vibrant, inclusive, and full of opportunities to connect. At DISV, we don't just learn together — we grow together.
