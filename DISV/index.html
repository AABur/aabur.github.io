Our Programmes

A home for every learner

From Early Years through Grade 12, DISV offers all three International Baccalaureate programmes — PYP, MYP, and DP — in a warm, multicultural community of 64 nationalities in the heart of Vienna. Where learning inspires, diversity strengthens, and every student belongs.
